LANGUAGE · AGENTS · NETWORKS

Information Programme Center, DSTA, Singapore

We study how language, artificial agents, and networked structures co-produce influence, deception, and collective behavior online; and build the tools to detect, simulate, and govern it.

Language

Psycholinguistic and narrative analysis of online discourse — stance, framing, and coordinated narratives across platforms and communities.

Focus: Language exploration across online communities · LLM-based studies · stance studies

Agents

Detecting and simulating synthetic and human-AI hybrid actors — from individual bots to large-scale multi-agent social simulations.

Focus: Social bot detection · social simulations

Networks

Structural and diffusion analysis of how narratives, influence, and coordination move through networked publics.

Focus: MASS framework · structural balance & triad analysis · narrative diffusion · information operations
