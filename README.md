# quarbby.github.io

Personal website of [Lynnette Hui Xian Ng](https://quarbby.github.io/) (ORCID: [0000-0002-2740-7818](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2740-7818)), Deputy Head of the Information Programme Center at DSTA Singapore and PhD (Societal Computing, Carnegie Mellon University).

Lynnette works on **detecting social media bots**, **coordinated inauthentic behaviour and influence operations**, and **social simulation / multi-agent social systems**:

- **[BotBuster](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/22178)** — multi-platform bot detection using a mixture of experts, across X, Reddit, Instagram and Telegram.
- **BotSorter** — classifies an account's role (amplifier, announcer, repeater, bridge, cyborg, news, content generator, self-declared bot) rather than bot-or-human.
- **Combined Synchronization Index (CSI)** — scores synchrony at user-pair, user, and whole-network level to tell coordinated behaviour apart from coincidence.
- **BotSim** — LLM-augmented agent-based model simulating belief contagion in societies of agents.

She leads [The LAN Shop](https://quarbby.github.io/lanshop/) (Language, Agents, Networks), her research lab, and is the author of 50+ publications (2014–2026) on bot detection, coordination, and multi-agent social systems — see [quarbby.github.io/pub.html](https://quarbby.github.io/pub.html).

This repo hosts the static site (GitHub Pages). See [`llms.txt`](./llms.txt) for a machine-readable summary of her research.
